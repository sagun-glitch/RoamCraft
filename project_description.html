1. Executive Summary

RoamCraft is an AI-powered vacation planning platform designed as a digital expedition dossier and field cartographer's trail map. Built with Node.js, Express, and Google Gemini, it streams personalized day-by-day itineraries live via Server-Sent Events (SSE) across a topographic contour-line canvas with live compass bearing calculations.

2. Design Tokens & Metaphor

  • Trail Permit Panel: Left registration card styled on aged parchment (#E8DCC0) with stamped toggle controls and handwritten field margin notes.
  • Topographic Map Canvas: Deep pine (#1B2A22) page background with SVG contour hairline rules (#C9B98C).
  • Animated Route Line & Compass Rose: Copper trail path (#B5622E) that animates across the map as itineraries compile, paired with a rotating live compass needle.
  • Typography: Header typography in Space Grotesk, data/body in IBM Plex Mono, and hand-lettered margin notes in Caveat.
